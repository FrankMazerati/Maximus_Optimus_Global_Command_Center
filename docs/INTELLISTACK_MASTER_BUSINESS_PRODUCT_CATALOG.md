## Catalog rules
- The hamburger menu is organized into expandable groups; users open a group, then select the destination.
- Keep top-level navigation compact. Put related tools into nested groups rather than listing every capability at the first level.
- Shared infrastructure is implemented once and reused by products.
- A menu entry is not proof of a working feature. Distinguish **Existing UI/demo**, **Database foundation**, **Planned**, and **Operational verified**.
- The parent brand and shared layout stay consistent; each product can have a distinct visual identity. **IntelliChat: purple and white** is the user's stated preference. Other product colors remain unapproved until the catalog is reconciled.
- Product names that were discussed but not confirmed as final remain in the catalog with a naming-review note.

## Shared service architecture and implementation dependencies

1. Security remediation and authorization tests.
2. Tenant/organization lifecycle and identity provisioning.
3. Product catalog reconciliation and authoritative entitlements.
4. Billing provider integration only after business approval.
5. IntelliLink server-side connector execution.
6. Workflow authorization, retries, idempotency and approval gates.
7. Scoped notifications and delivery status.
8. Audit event integrity, coverage and retention.
9. IntelliVision reporting after tenant scoping is proven.
10. Maximus Optimus tool access only after authorization and audit controls are proven.

## Navigation implementation guidance
- Use expandable/collapsible menu groups with clear labels and nested sections.
- Preserve mobile usability, independent menu scrolling, keyboard focus, and the existing responsive shell.
- Keep frequently used destinations near the top; put administrative and specialized tools under their domain.
- Keep “Log out” in the account area rather than repeating it after every group.
- Where a feature has no implemented page yet, label it **Planned** or **Setup required** and do not imply it is operational.
- Do not route multiple unrelated menu entries to one placeholder page without explaining the limitation.

## Operational acceptance rule
Only label a product **Operational verified** after its real user flow, persistence, server-side authorization, cross-tenant denial tests, error handling, audit behavior, and deployment smoke test have passed. Menu visibility alone is not an acceptance test.


## Marketplace connector ecosystem extension
The IntelliStack Marketplace is intended to become a full product and connector ecosystem, not just a package picker. Role-aware collections include CFO finance/revenue tools (Salesforce, QuickBooks Online, Xero, NetSuite, Stripe, Power BI/Tableau), CIO engineering/infrastructure/identity/security tools (GitHub, GitLab, Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Entra ID, Okta, Intune, Sentinel, CrowdStrike, Datadog, ServiceNow, Jira), and CEO executive reporting/strategic operations tools (Power BI, Tableau, Salesforce, NetSuite, Asana, monday.com, Jira). These are recommendations only; authorization, connector entitlements, tenant scopes and provider connections remain separate.
