# 12. Organizational Memory & Lessons Learned

**Status:** Knowledge lifecycle framework; content must be reviewed before it becomes reusable guidance.

## Purpose
Preserve useful institutional knowledge across staff changes, projects, incidents, releases, decisions, and recurring work.

## What belongs here
Approved SOPs, troubleshooting results, runbooks, known issues, incident lessons, project history/inception, architecture decisions, build/version notes, release changes, feature explanations, prior decisions, and reusable organizational context.

## Capture walkthrough
1. Capture the problem or decision, context, date, product/version, owner, and source records.
2. Separate observed facts from interpretation and recommendation.
3. Remove secrets and unnecessary personal or tenant-specific data.
4. Have a subject-matter owner review accuracy, relevance, sensitivity, and reuse rights.
5. Convert useful results into a reusable article or runbook with prerequisites and verification.
6. Assign version, owner, review date, status, and audience.
7. Link the article to related tickets, incidents, changes, releases, and superseding articles where allowed.

## Lessons learned
A post-incident lesson should state what happened, impact, timeline, contributing factors, detection/response quality, corrective actions, owners, due dates, and what other teams can safely reuse. Avoid blame-oriented language and avoid generalizing from one unusual case without evidence.

## HelpBuddy retrieval
HelpBuddy should prefer current approved articles over stale drafts and should use the customer's permitted scope. Historical chat or ticket text is not automatically authoritative or suitable for broad publication. If two approved sources conflict, identify the conflict and route it to the content owner rather than silently choosing one.

## Lifecycle
Draft → subject-matter review → security/privacy review when needed → approval → publication to the right audience → periodic review → revision or retirement. Preserve provenance and change history.
