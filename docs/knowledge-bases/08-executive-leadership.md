# 8. CEO, Executive & Leadership Knowledge

**Status:** Framework for an organization-owned executive knowledge base; populate only with approved records.

## Purpose
Give authorized leaders a trustworthy view of business priorities, risk, operational health, decisions, and issues requiring executive judgment.

## Knowledge domains
- Strategic objectives and approved plans.
- Executive-level service and incident summaries.
- Business continuity and crisis decision procedures.
- Risk register, accepted exceptions, and accountable owners.
- Decision records, approvals, commitments, and review dates.
- Organization-specific escalation criteria and communications templates.
- Approved KPI definitions and reporting cadence.

## Executive walkthrough
1. Confirm the leader's identity and executive scope.
2. Review the reporting period, data sources, last-refresh time, and any missing inputs.
3. Separate verified metrics from estimates, forecasts, and unresolved questions.
4. Review decisions required, options, impact, risk, deadline, and accountable owner.
5. Record decisions and delegated actions in the authoritative approval/task system.
6. Track execution and return unresolved or overdue items to the appropriate owner.

## CEO authority and limits
The CEO may hold business decision authority, but that does not automatically grant unrestricted access to every employee's personal information, security evidence, credentials, or legally restricted records. Platform Global Admin is a separate technical/governance role. Document delegation and approval boundaries for each organization.

## Confidentiality
Executive plans, board material, financial details, personnel matters, and crisis communications should be access-classified. HelpBuddy must retrieve only the portions the current user is authorized to see. Avoid putting confidential details in general knowledge articles or broad summaries.

## Reporting standard
Every executive brief should identify period, source, status, impact, trend where supported, decisions needed, owner, due date, and confidence/limitations. Do not present missing data as zero or imply a forecast is a confirmed result.
