# 16. Premium HelpBuddy Knowledge

**Status:** Proposed entitlement-gated knowledge collection. Exact package access must be mapped to the approved product catalog before enabling.

## Purpose
Offer advanced HelpBuddy workflows and specialized operational guidance to entitled customers and authorized users without weakening role-level security.

## Potential content
- Advanced multi-step workflows and workflow composition.
- Specialized troubleshooting and cross-module diagnostics.
- Advanced ticket/incident summarization and prioritization.
- Automation design, approval gates, exception handling, and audit interpretation.
- Advanced connector and migration guidance when included in the customer's package.
- Advanced reporting, operational insights, and admin-oriented guidance.
- Feature-specific examples, limitations, release notes, and validated templates.

## Entitlement and authorization
Access requires both the organization's entitlement for the relevant feature and the user's permission to view/use it. Sensitive operational records require their own authorization. Premium subscription does not grant every employee access to executive, security, audit, or platform-administration material.

## Workflow walkthrough
1. Check feature entitlement and user role.
2. Confirm prerequisites and supported versions.
3. Explain the workflow, its impact, and any approval requirements.
4. Gather the minimum necessary context from authorized sources.
5. Run only approved actions through registered workflows.
6. Verify outputs, show unresolved exceptions, and record relevant audit references.
7. Provide rollback or escalation steps for failures.

## Quality bar
Advanced articles should include intended outcome, prerequisites, scope, tested version/environment, expected result, failure paths, permissions, approval gates, audit behavior, and owner. Label untested examples as examples—not production-ready runbooks.

## Product governance
The entitlement matrix is configuration-driven and must align with the actual approved packages. Do not hardcode pricing or promise a premium feature in customer-facing documentation until product ownership confirms its availability.
