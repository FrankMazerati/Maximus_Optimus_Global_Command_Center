# 17. IntelliSphere Product & Service Catalog

**Status:** Initial catalog knowledge draft based on project conversations. Product names and capabilities marked **confirm** must be reconciled with the current product registry before being published as promises to customers.

## Purpose
Give HelpBuddy, users, administrators, and prospective customers clear explanations of the IntelliStack / Maximus Optimus ecosystem, what each product is for, how products relate, who uses them, and what is currently available versus planned.

## IntelliSphere role
In this project, **IntelliSphere** is the proposed umbrella knowledge experience/catalog for explaining products, services, modules, packages, integrations, and how they work together. Confirm whether IntelliSphere is the official customer-facing name and whether it is a product, portal, or knowledge-base label before final branding.

## Core platform: Maximus Optimus Global Command Center
The intended platform is a unified operational environment connecting people, IT, security, incidents, service requests, tickets, tasks, communications, documents, scheduling, workflows, approvals, audit, organizational knowledge, and AI-assisted operations. HelpBuddy is intended to be the conversational operational interface. The full scope is a product direction; individual modules and integrations must be checked for implementation and entitlement.

### HelpBuddy
AI-powered operational assistant and guide. Intended to help authorized users find knowledge, understand work, retrieve permitted ticket/incident context, follow approved runbooks, and interact with supported workflows. It must verify identity and authorization, respect tenant boundaries, and report action outcomes truthfully.

### IntelliChat
Proposed/related collaboration and chat capability within the Command Center ecosystem. Confirm the current module's available messaging, channel, history, and integration features before promising specific behavior.

### IntelliCalendar
Calendar and scheduling capability referenced in the platform plan. Intended to support schedules and meeting workflows where connected and enabled. Confirm current provider integrations and write capabilities.

### Knowledge Base
Shared governed knowledge service supporting SOPs, troubleshooting, runbooks, policies, known incidents, project history, version/build information, features, updates, and organizational memory. Knowledge is filtered by user permissions, tenant scope, sensitivity, and subscription entitlements.

### Tickets, Tasks, Incidents, War Rooms, Documents, Approvals, Signatures, Audit, Network, Security, and AI
These are named platform work areas in the product vision. Their actual state, actions, and availability must be verified in the target deployment. Explain each using its actual configured functionality rather than inferring completion from the roadmap.

## Product page template
For each product/service, maintain: canonical name; short description; customer problem solved; intended users; key capabilities; prerequisites; integrations; data handled; permissions; package/entitlement; status (available, tested, in development, planned, custom required); onboarding walkthrough; limitations; support/escalation route; release/version; owner; source of truth; last review.

## HelpBuddy response standard
When asked “What is [product]?”, explain the approved purpose, target user, current confirmed capabilities, prerequisites, and how to begin. Distinguish confirmed product behavior from roadmap concepts. If a name or feature is ambiguous, say it needs confirmation rather than guessing.
