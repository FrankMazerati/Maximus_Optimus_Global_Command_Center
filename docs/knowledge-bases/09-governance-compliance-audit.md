# 9. Governance, Compliance, Audit & Risk

**Status:** Governance framework; applicable laws, standards, retention periods, and control owners must be set by the customer.

## Purpose
Maintain evidence that platform and organizational work follows approved policy, access rules, change controls, retention requirements, and risk decisions.

## Governance walkthrough
1. Identify the applicable policy, control, system, owner, and scope.
2. Confirm the current approved version and effective date.
3. Collect evidence from authoritative records, with provenance and timestamps.
4. Record exceptions with rationale, approver, compensating controls, expiration, and review owner.
5. Track remediation items to closure and retain evidence according to policy.
6. Review access to audit material and avoid altering original evidence.
7. Prepare findings that distinguish facts, interpretations, gaps, and recommendations.

## Audit trail minimums
For consequential actions, capture actor/service identity, timestamp, target record, action, authorization context, result, and correlation/reference ID where available. Do not log passwords, tokens, private keys, or unnecessary sensitive payloads.

## Risk register
Each risk should have a description, likelihood/impact assessment method, owner, mitigation, due date, residual risk, acceptance authority, and next review. Risk scoring must follow a documented organizational method rather than arbitrary numbers.

## HelpBuddy rules
HelpBuddy can help locate policies and assemble authorized evidence. It must not self-certify compliance, fabricate audit evidence, or mark a control effective without the required validation. Keep internal control implementation details separate from customer-facing explanations when disclosure could create risk.

## Maintenance
Assign each control a responsible owner, evidence source, test frequency, last-tested date, result, and remediation link. Validate the required retention and deletion behavior with legal/compliance owners before configuring it.
