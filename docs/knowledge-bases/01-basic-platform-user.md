# 1. Basic Platform & User Knowledge

**Status:** Initial walkthrough; verify screen labels and enabled modules in the target environment.

## Purpose
Help everyday users navigate the Global Command Center and understand the standard work cycle without granting administrative privileges.

## Intended audience
Employees, contractors, job seekers where applicable, team leads, and other authenticated users. Users see only the modules, records, and actions authorized for their role, organization, tenant, and subscription.

## Before you begin
- Sign in through the approved authentication method.
- Confirm the displayed identity and organization are correct.
- If a module is missing, check role assignment and package entitlement with an administrator; do not attempt to bypass access controls.

## Basic walkthrough
1. Open the Command Center home/dashboard and review the modules made available to your role.
2. Use navigation/search to locate work areas such as HelpBuddy, tickets, tasks, calendar, activity, documents, or other enabled modules.
3. Open HelpBuddy and state the goal, relevant ticket or incident identifier if known, and what outcome you need.
4. Review the response. For operational requests, confirm the target record and proposed action before any consequential change.
5. Track assigned tasks, ticket updates, approvals, and notifications in the relevant source module.
6. Before leaving, make sure important work is saved and that no action is still pending or has an unknown outcome.

## Ask HelpBuddy effectively
Prefer: “Find my open tickets,” “Explain the steps for this approved task,” or “Summarize ticket [ID] that I have access to.” Provide only the information needed. Do not paste passwords, API keys, payment details, or unnecessary personal data.

## Common troubleshooting
- **Cannot sign in:** verify the account and approved login path; use the account-recovery process or contact support.
- **Module unavailable:** the module may be disabled, outside your entitlement, or restricted by role.
- **Record not found:** confirm the identifier and organization context; do not assume the record was deleted.
- **Action appears stuck:** check the current record state before retrying to avoid duplicate submissions.
- **Information seems wrong:** compare against the authoritative record and report the discrepancy with a timestamp and safe reference.

## Verification
A successful task is confirmed by the authoritative module showing the expected saved state, not merely by a conversational confirmation.

## Boundaries
This guide does not grant access, promise that every named module is live, or replace organization-specific policy.
