# 13. Business Operations & Department Knowledge

**Status:** Reusable template. Each organization should add its own department-specific policies and approved workflows.

## Purpose
Help employees and managers find consistent procedures for nontechnical and cross-functional work while respecting department confidentiality.

## Potential domains
Human resources, finance, vendor management, procurement, facilities, project operations, customer service, service delivery, communications, scheduling, document management, approvals, and signatures. Include only domains enabled for the organization.

## Procedure walkthrough
1. Identify the request type and responsible department.
2. Check the current approved policy, eligibility, required fields, and approval path.
3. Confirm requester authority and whether the action requires manager, finance, HR, legal, or executive approval.
4. Gather only necessary information and route it through the authorized workflow.
5. Track the request in its system of record.
6. Verify approval or completion before communicating the outcome.
7. Retain evidence and records according to the relevant policy.

## Examples
- A meeting request should identify attendees, purpose, duration, and scheduling constraints, then use the calendar workflow if available.
- A purchase request should follow budget and approval policy; HelpBuddy must not imply that a purchase is approved until the authoritative approval record confirms it.
- An HR question should retrieve the policy relevant to the user's region and role and route confidential personnel matters only to authorized HR personnel.
- A document/signature request should verify the document version, signatories, authority, and completion state.

## HelpBuddy boundaries
Do not expose employee records, compensation, personal information, confidential vendor terms, or financial data to unauthorized users. Do not provide legal, tax, or HR determinations as authoritative when a qualified owner must decide. State when a workflow or integration is not enabled.

## Local configuration
Each process needs an owner, purpose, eligible requester, prerequisites, steps, approvals, SLA where applicable, exceptions, evidence, data classification, and escalation route.
