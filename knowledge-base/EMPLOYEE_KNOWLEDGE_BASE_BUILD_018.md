# Employee Knowledge Base — Build 018 Baseline

This is a documentation baseline for the Employee Knowledge Base. It does not imply that a searchable knowledge-base interface or AI retrieval is already connected.

## Article standards
- Give each article a title, purpose, audience, owner, revision date, and review status.
- Prefer approved troubleshooting steps, clear prerequisites, expected results, escalation criteria, and rollback guidance.
- Distinguish verified fixes from suggestions and sample/demo data.
- Do not include passwords, secrets, private end-user information, or restricted administrator notes in general employee articles.

## Initial collections
1. **Troubleshooting & approved solutions** — symptom, impact, checks, approved fix, validation, escalation.
2. **How-to guides** — purpose, access required, numbered steps, expected result, recovery path.
3. **Onboarding & FAQs** — approved procedures, common terms, role-specific orientation.
4. **Training & qualifications** — skill matrix, training path, lab exercise, qualification evidence, required authorization for advanced assignments.
5. **Developer & system documentation** — architecture, module specifications, API/data contracts, deployment procedures, known defects, release notes, and integration status.

## HelpBuddy usage rules
HelpBuddy should surface approved, relevant articles, preserve the article revision in task context, and clearly label uncertain or unapproved suggestions. It must not bypass role authorization or treat a knowledge article as permission to perform restricted work.

## Current state
Build 018 adds this written baseline only. Search indexing, article management UI, live HelpBuddy retrieval, and access enforcement remain unverified/not implemented by this documentation change.
